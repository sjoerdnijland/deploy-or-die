1
📋
ALLOCATE TOKENS
Discuss within your team and fill in your scorecard. Allocate all your tokens across the seven domains. Decide whether to train a specialist (zero tokens in that domain this round) or invest in an architectural upgrade (costs 2 tokens, earns +3 pts). Negotiate cross-team training if needed. Hand scorecard to GM when done.
2
🔒
LOCK IN ON SCREEN
Each team enters their allocation on the shared screen and clicks Lock In. Results stay hidden. While the GM collects scorecards, each team receives one incident card to read and discuss: What went wrong? What should the team have done?
3
💬
INCIDENT DISCUSSION
Each team discusses their incident card for 3–5 minutes. Use the discussion question on the card. Share real-life examples if you have them. The GM applies training level-ups from the collected scorecards during this time.
4
🎲
REVEAL ALL RESULTS
The last team clicks Reveal All Results. All team scores appear simultaneously. Compare allocations and outcomes. Who defended what? Who took risks? Who invested in architecture or training?
5
📚
RETRO — DEVOPS PRINCIPLE
The GM draws one principle card for this round and reads it aloud. Short group discussion: Does this connect to what just happened? Has anyone lived this? Keep it to 5 minutes, then start the next round.
Tokens: Base budget + 1 per trained role (L1+) + upgrades. Morale below 3 = −1 token. Morale below 1 = training benefits suspended. · Incident cards are pre-sorted per round — draw from the correct round's stack. · Principle cards are grouped per round — draw one per retro.
