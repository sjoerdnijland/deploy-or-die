DEPLOY OR DIE
GAME MASTER MANUAL · CONFIDENTIAL
6 ROUNDS · 2–6 TEAMS · 60–90 MIN
🎯 PURPOSE OF THE GAME

Each team manages a growing software product through six rounds. Every round they allocate limited tokens across seven domains — features, security, stability, compliance, performance, tech debt, and morale. Then incidents hit.

The goal is to score the highest cumulative points across six rounds by making smart investment decisions: defend against incidents, seize opportunities, train specialists, and invest in architecture — all under real resource constraints.

The deeper learning goal: teams experience the consequences of DevOps decisions first-hand — deferred security, ignored debt, and low morale have visible, compounding effects. The game makes abstract principles concrete through consequence, not instruction.

Role levels: L1 = +1 free token in domain each round. L2 = halves defence threshold (or special benefit). Set levels on the retro screen after collecting scorecards.

5TRAINING

A team can train a specialist by writing it on their scorecard (Round, Team, Role). They score zero tokens in that domain that round — you apply this when entering scores.

Cross-team training: Another team can train that specialist instead, at a cost of 1 token from the hosting team's pool. Both teams note it on their cards. The training still counts — you apply the level-up on the retro screen.

Training is permanent. Each training action raises the role by one level (max L2). +1 morale per completed training — apply on retro screen.

6MORALE

Starts at 5/10. The screen tracks this automatically.

Rises: +1 per morale token allocated (max +3), +1 per opportunity seized, +1 per training completed.

Falls: −1 per undefended critical incident, −1 per 10 debt accrued, −1 if zero morale tokens, −1 per role untrained 3+ rounds.

Below 3: −1 capacity token that round. Below 1: training benefits suspended.

7ARCHITECTURAL UPGRADES
🤝Cross-Functional Teams+1 capacity token per round
🔗MicroservicesStability incidents: −1 token to defend
🔁CI/CD Pipeline+2 pts/round if any trained role
🔐DevSecOps / Zero TrustSecurity incidents: −1 token to defend
📊Observability PlatformSRE gets +1 free Stability token
Cost: 2 tokens from the buying team's pool. Only one team can buy each upgrade. Buying team gets +3 pts bonus and the benefit that same round. All other teams get the benefit from the next round onward. Teams must coordinate — only one buyer per upgrade for the whole game.
8SCORING REFERENCE

Features: ×9 pts per token (multiplier drops if tech debt is neglected in later rounds).

Incidents defended: +35% of incident's debt value as bonus pts.

Incidents hit: −debt value as penalty + debt accrued.

Opportunities seized: +18–22 pts if threshold met.

Stability bonus: +5 pts if ≥2 stability tokens allocated.

Morale investment: +3 pts if ≥2 morale tokens allocated.

Domain lock threshold: if accrued debt in a domain exceeds 25 AND that domain is hit again — the domain locks. A trained specialist (L1+) must unlock it (costs 1 token, unlocks next round).

9THE RETRO
After the reveal, the retro screen shows the DevOps principle for that round. Collect scorecards. Apply training level-ups on screen. Then ask the table: "Has anyone experienced this in real life? What was the impact?" — this is the learning moment. Keep it to 3–5 minutes per round.
